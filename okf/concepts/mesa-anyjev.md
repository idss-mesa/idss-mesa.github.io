# mesa-anyjev

Makes MESA's ontology and schema choices declarative and
calibrated.[^mesa-anyjev-repo] Decisions are AnyJev typed decisions (choice,
yes/no, score) over [mesa-mcp](mesa-mcp.md)'s OBO/OLS ontology tools. Every
decision is recorded in a provenance sidecar next to the
[mesa-ducklake](mesa-ducklake.md) AVU history. Hosted Jev through MotherDuck
`prompt_jev` is available as a policy-gated third provider. Version 0.1.0.

Documentation, published as its own OKF v0.2 bundle, is at
<https://idss-mesa.github.io/mesa-anyjev/>:[^mesa-anyjev-docs]

- Outline: <https://idss-mesa.github.io/mesa-anyjev/llms.txt>
- Full corpus: <https://idss-mesa.github.io/mesa-anyjev/llms-full.txt>
- Agent guide: <https://idss-mesa.github.io/mesa-anyjev/about/ai-agents/>

Part of MESA (Multidisciplinary Environment for Scientific Advancement),
an NSF IDSS Category II project at the University of New Mexico; see
[MESA](home.md). Organization: <https://github.com/idss-mesa>.
