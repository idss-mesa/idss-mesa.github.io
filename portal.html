The MESA Portal. Sign in and start.

Sign in to mesa.cyverse.org with a free CyVerse account to browse, share, and describe your Data Store files, launch apps, and follow your analyses. The featured apps start from the portal with your Data Store mounted and the MESA MCP servers already registered for Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, and Antigravity.
